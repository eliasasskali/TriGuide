Privacy Policy

Debonk · Last updated: May 2025

Debonk ("the app", "we", "our") is a triathlon and duathlon training app developed as an independent project. This policy explains what data is collected when you use Debonk, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

1. No account required

Debonk does not require you to create an account. No name, email address, or personal credentials are collected or stored by us.

2. Data collected automatically

Debonk uses third-party services to help us understand app performance and fix issues. These services may collect the following data automatically:

  • Crash reports - via Firebase Crashlytics. When the app crashes, diagnostic data including device model, OS version, and the state of the app at the time of the crash is sent to Google. This data is used solely to identify and fix bugs.
  • Usage analytics - via Firebase Analytics. Anonymised data about which features are used and how the app is navigated is collected. This helps us improve the app experience. This data is not linked to your identity.
  • Device information - including device type, operating system version, and app version, collected by Firebase as part of the above services.
  • A Firebase installation ID - a randomly generated identifier used by Firebase to distinguish devices. This is not linked to any personal identity.

We do not use this data for advertising and we do not sell it to any third party.

3. Training and health data

Any training data you enter in Debonk (workouts, carbohydrate goals, session notes) is stored locally on your device only. This data is not transmitted to our servers or to any third party.

Debonk provides general training guidance and nutritional estimates for informational purposes only. It is not a medical application and does not provide medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before making decisions about your health or nutrition.

4. Third-party services

Debonk uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Analytics - Google LLC
  • Firebase Crashlytics - Google LLC

5. Children's privacy

Debonk is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

6. Data retention

Crash and analytics data collected via Firebase is retained according to Google's standard retention policies. Local data stored on your device can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the app.

7. Your rights

Depending on your country of residence, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of data associated with your device. To exercise these rights or to ask any questions about this policy, please contact us at the email below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
